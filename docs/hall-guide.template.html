Point Hub — Hall Guide

Set up the LED scoreboard and run a match. Follow the steps in order; don't skip the green checks.

The one rule

The scoreboard is self-contained: it makes its own wifi and runs everything itself. There's no separate box. Turn it on, join its wifi with your phone (scan the codes below; they're also on the board's own screen until someone connects), and score. Your phone must be on that wifi, not on mobile data. No internet is needed to score.

2 · Setting up — in this order

  1. Stand or hang the board where players can see it. Plug it into mains.
    It boots itself. There's nothing to switch on separately.
    ✓ You should see: a gold POINT HUB splash, then after ~45s the KSC Wiedikon crest with a "Join WiFi" QR on the left and an "Open UI" QR on the right. Under a minute is normal, so don't touch anything before then.
  2. Scan SCAN 1 (or the left code on the board) to join the wifi. Turn mobile data OFF.
    The wifi has no internet. That's normal. Phones jump back to mobile data unless you stop them.
  3. Scan SCAN 2 (or the right code on the board) to open Point Hub. If it shows Tap to start, tap it.
    That one tap puts the page in full screen and landscape, and keeps the screen awake for the match. No such screen (iPhone)? Turn the phone sideways and tap the sun button (Keep awake). Bookmark the page for next time.
    The QR codes disappear from the board once anyone is on the controller: the board shows the crest and the clock instead. That's normal, not a fault. Use this sheet.
    Club tablet: just open the Point Hub app. It finds the board by itself, opens full screen and keeps the screen awake, so you can skip steps 2 and 3. It also keeps a copy of every set and match on the tablet.
  4. Type the scorer PIN when it asks ("Scoreboard locked").
    The PIN screen covers the whole page until it's entered. This phone remembers it after that. Get it wrong 5 times and it locks you out for 30s, doubling up to 5 minutes. That's the board protecting itself, not a fault. Wait it out.
    "Set a scorer PIN" instead? This board has no PIN yet (the first evening after an update). Choose 1–8 digits, type them twice, tap Set PIN and write it down for the coaches. Nobody on the wifi can score until a PIN is set. Once set, it can be changed but not removed, and it cannot be looked up.
    On the cable: a tablet plugged into the board's network cable is never asked for the PIN.
  5. Pick the sport.
    This screen opens every time. Tapping the sport that's already current just moves on, and nothing restarts. Tapping a different one restarts the board (~8 seconds). The same screen also has Diagnostics.
    ✓ You should see: "Choose the sport", with the current one tagged current.
  6. Pick what to do: Continue game, New game, or Delete saved game.
    Continue only appears if the board is holding a match. It survives a power cut or a restart, so if the plug got kicked, this is how you get the score back. If it says the match is from an earlier day, pick New game.
    ✓ You should see: the scoreboard: team cards, big + / −, and the tabs Game · Link · History across the top.
  7. Tonight's home game: Link tab → Today at the hall → tap the game → Start game.
    Both team names go on the board, home on the left. The board shows the team names and the time while the teams arrive, and the Game tab says "Ready: HOME vs AWAY" with two buttons: Start official warm-up (10 min) (the match starts when the warm-up ends) or Start match now. An hour before, the controller also offers it by itself: "Tonight's game: … Set up / Not now", or with several home games, "Which game is on this board?". Nothing goes on the board until you tap. Not on the list? Type the names on the Game tab instead.
  8. Check the header: it should say Board ✓. Then check the names and colours.
    Board ✗ = see Trouble 1. Long names shrink to fit automatically, and the − / + beside a name sets its size on the panel. If two colours would look alike on the board, or one is too dark for the LEDs, a line under the team cards says so: tap its Try … button.
    ✓ You should see: under the team cards, "Seen from the court": a live copy of the panel, the way the hall sees it. If you sit behind the board, your team cards are swapped to match your view (Settings → Board identity → "Scorer sits behind the board").

3 · Running the match — and your live test

Do these in order the first time; it doubles as a full check that everything works.

Do thisWhat happens
Tap + on a teamThe point appears and blinks in that team's colour; the serve indicator moves to that side.
Tap − (a correction)The point drops. It does not blink: only real points blink. On the team that has just won a set, − takes the set back too.
Undo (bottom bar)Takes back the last thing entered: a point, a timeout, a sub, the serve, a swap, a new set. The button says what it will undo (e.g. "Point KSCW"). Tap again for the one before.
Tap a number (score, sets, T/O, sub)A little editor opens: type the exact value, press Set. For fixing a miscount fast. Typed values don't blink.
T/O + for a teamGoes in at once, with no question. The panel shows TIME OUT with that team underneath, plus a clock: 30s for indoor volleyball, 60s for beach and basketball. At zero the horn sounds. End it early with ✕ (no horn, deliberately). Mis-tap? The note at the bottom has Undo for 6 seconds.
T/O + again (to the limit)That counter turns dark red: none left. Indoor gets 2, beach 1, basketball 5.
Sub +Goes in at once, with the same Undo note. The counter blinks; amber one short of the limit, dark red at it. Indoor volleyball only: beach has no subs, and basketball shows team fouls here instead.
Win the setThe winning point blinks, the board holds the final score, and the phone shows a full-screen SET ENDED · Start interval →. Wrong point? Use Undo before you tap through. Tapping through swaps ends, resets the points, timeouts and subs, and runs the INTERVAL clock (3 min indoor) → Start next set →.
Deciding set, first to 8The phone asks "Switch sides?". On yes, the whole panel says COURT SWITCH in gold for ~3s and the ends swap behind it.
Win the matchThe phone asks "Match finished — show the result?". The result shows the winner, the score and every set, with New game. The board shows the result screen (winner in gold) until you start the next game. Said "Not now"? Tap the Final chip in the header.
Match log (bottom bar)The last thing entered, with its time. Tap it for the whole match: every point, timeout and sub, with times.
Warm-upFull-screen WARM UP clock (10 min). Horn at zero.
VS · H / T-VS puts the team names and the time on the board. H / T- switches the time to the countdown to the game; the score is kept, and the next point brings the scoreboard back.
Switch sides (manual)Teams change ends: names, colours and scores all move together.
Reset matchThrows the match away and starts again from 0–0 (asks first). An unfinished match does not appear in History.
History tabFinished matches with per-set scores. Export CSV or JSON.
Settings (gear)Each section has its own Save button. It turns blue when there's something unsaved, and nothing saves by itself. Includes brightness, countdowns, horn, match format, the crest screen, PIN, power and the hall internet. Theme (Light · Dark · E-ink for bright sun) changes this phone only, at once.

Beach: sets to 21 (15 decider), no substitutions, one timeout each, an amber SWITCH cue on the panel every 7 points (5 in the decider), and an automatic 1-minute technical timeout at 21 total points that swaps ends. A new beach game asks for the serve order (who serves first, each pair's first server); the board follows it after that, and PLAYER switches the server by hand. Basketball: +1 / +2 / +3 buttons instead of one +, a PERIOD box (Q1–Q4, OT, FINAL) instead of the set line, End period, team fouls (amber at 4, dark red at the 5th = bonus), and the serve arrow becomes possession. Simple scoreboard: two numbers and no rules, for anything else. It isn't kept in History.

Keep in mind

The score is safe: it lives on the board, not the phone, so you can lock the phone, swap phones, or hand over. Nothing is lost. More than one phone can be connected; they all show the same score. Keep one person scoring (a second scorer double-counts). Keep the phone on the ledbox_C0270 wifi. Wander out of range and the page pauses; walk back and it reconnects. If the screen still goes dark, tap the sun button (Keep awake) once, or set the phone's display timeout to Never. A note "Board clock corrected" is normal: your phone sets the board's clock.

4 · Packing up

  1. Settings → Power → Shut down (confirm).
    Halts the board cleanly, which protects the memory card. Never just pull the plug while it's running.
    ✓ You should see: "Now press the power button to switch it off", and the panel goes dark within a few seconds.
  2. Unplug the board from mains.
    Only after the panel has gone dark. It starts itself next time, with nothing to launch by hand.
  3. Put the phone back on its normal wifi / mobile data. Coil the cable.

Between matches? Don't shut down. VS puts the team names and the time on the board. To make the panel dark for a while, set Settings → Display → Panel brightness to 0: the controller stays up, so you're one tap from being live again.

5 · Scoring on an OpenVolley laptop — optional

When the match is scored on the electronic scoresheet (OpenVolley on a laptop), the board can follow it, so nobody taps the phone. Put the laptop on the board's wifi ledbox_C0270 (or on the cable) and open the match there. Then on the phone: Link tab → Live matches on this network → ↻ Refresh matches → tap the match. The board now shows the scoresheet's score, and its timeouts and set intervals run the board's own TIME OUT and INTERVAL clocks, horn included, by themselves. Don't score on the phone as well.

Match not in the list? Check the laptop is on ledbox_C0270 with the match open, then tap ↻ Refresh matches again. For a game started from the schedule, Match info on the Game tab shows the team lists (numbers, names, licences) to copy into the scoresheet. It needs live scoring on and the scorer PIN.

6 · Hall internet and live scores — optional

The scoreboard never needs the internet. With it, the board also shows the score live on wiedisync.kscw.ch/live (Settings → Board identity → Connect to live scoring), updates the season schedule and sets its own clock. The hall's free wifi lets the board out only after a login with a code by SMS, which lasts about a day. When the Game tab says "Hall Wi-Fi needs a login": tap Log in → type your mobile number → Send code → type the code from the SMS → Log in. The number is used for this login only and is not saved on the board. The same login is in Settings → Hall internet.

✓ You should see: "Online — logged in until …". When the login is about to run out, the header says Wi-Fi until HH:MM. No internet at all? The scoreboard works the same; Today at the hall then uses the schedule the board saved the last time it was online ("offline — last update …"), or you type the names on the Game tab.

7 · Troubleshooting

Work down the list. Nine times out of ten it's the first two rows.

ProblemWhat it meansWhat to do
Trouble 1 — "Board not connected" (header: Board ✗)The controller is fine; it can't reach the panel. Your scores are still being recorded. Right after power-on the display takes a few extra seconds. Wait ~30s and it clears on its own. Still there after a minute → Settings → Power → Restart board and wait ~45s. Still nothing → off at the mains, wait 10s, on.
Trouble 2 — "Not connected — taps are not reaching the board" / "Frozen at HH:MM:SS"The opposite problem: your phone has lost the controller. The buttons grey out; nothing on screen is live. Don't touch the board: it's probably fine. Check the wifi says ledbox_C0270, mobile data OFF, walk closer; the page recovers by itself. A tap that said "Not sent" may not have counted: at "Connection back", compare the board with the phone and fix it with Undo or by tapping the number.
Trouble 3 — Page won't open at allPhone isn't on the board's wifi, or slipped to mobile data. Check the wifi says ledbox_C0270. Turn mobile data OFF. Reload 172.24.1.1:8890. Wifi not listed at all → the board isn't powered or is still booting (wait for the crest).
Trouble 3b — Club tablet: "Looking for the scoreboard…"The app can't reach the board. The board is almost certainly fine. Is the board on? Is the tablet on ledbox_C0270? Tap Connect to scoreboard Wi-Fi or Retry. Still stuck → open Chrome and type 172.24.1.1:8890 (SCAN 2 is the same address).
Trouble 4 — PIN screens"Set a scorer PIN": the board has none yet. "Too many wrong PINs — try again in Ns": the lockout. No PIN yet → choose one (1–8 digits, twice) → Set PIN, and write it down for the coaches. Lockout → 5 tries, then 30s, doubling to 5 minutes: wait it out. The PIN cannot be looked up. Lost it? A tablet on the cable still scores without it; for a new PIN, ask the club.
Trouble 5 — Board stuck on an old scoreThe link dropped and came back. Wait ~20 seconds: the board checks itself and repaints. Don't tap + then − to "wake it up": at set point that + really ends the set. If it happened, use Undo.
Trouble 6 — Part of the panel dark, flickering or the wrong colourPossibly a hardware fault in one of the three panel modules. (A team colour in light grey is only too dark for the LEDs: tap Try … under the team cards.) Between matches: Diagnostics → Panel test → Show test screen. The panel numbers its modules 1 | 2 | 3, each with a white frame and red, green, blue and white bars. Report the number of a module that is dark, has a gap in its frame or misses a bar. It goes back by itself after 20 seconds.
Trouble 7 — No hornThe horn is off, or not working. Diagnostics → Panel test → Test horn. Nothing → Settings → Countdowns → "Horn when a countdown ends" must be on (then Save).
Trouble 8 — Blank page / missing buttonsStale page in the phone browser. "Update ready" at the bottom → Reload now. Otherwise pull to refresh, or close the tab and scan SCAN 2 again.
Trouble 9 — Nothing worksStart from zero. Off at the mains, count to ten, on again. Wait ~45s for the crest, rejoin the wifi, reopen the page. Your match is not lost: pick Continue game.
Trouble 10 — Something else, or it keeps happeningTell the club, with the board's own report. Diagnostics tab (also on the sport picker) → live log → Export. Send that file with a line on what happened and when.
