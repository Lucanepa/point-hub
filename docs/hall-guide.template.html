Point Hub — Hall Guide

Set up the LED scoreboard and run a match. Follow the steps in order; don't skip the green checks.

The one rule

The scoreboard is self-contained — it makes its own wifi and runs everything itself. There's no separate box and no cable to plug in. Turn it on, join its wifi with your phone (scan the codes below — they're also on the board's own screen until someone connects), and score. Your phone must be on that wifi, not on mobile data.

2 · Setting up — in this order

  1. Stand or hang the board where players can see it. Plug it into mains.
    It boots itself — nothing to switch on separately.
    ✓ You should see: a gold POINT HUB splash, then after ~45s the KSC Wiedikon crest with a "Join WiFi" QR on the left and an "Open UI" QR on the right. Under a minute is normal — don't touch anything before then.
  2. Scan SCAN 1 (or the left code on the board) to join the wifi. Turn mobile data OFF.
    The wifi has no internet — that's normal. Phones jump back to mobile data unless you stop them.
  3. Scan SCAN 2 (or the right code on the board) to open Point Hub. Bookmark it.
    The QR codes disappear from the board once anyone opens the controller — they're replaced by a big clock. That's normal, not a fault. Scan them before you open the page, or use this sheet.
  4. If it asks for the scorer PIN, type it.
    With a PIN set, the PIN screen is the whole page — nobody can even watch until it's entered. Get it wrong 5 times and it locks you out for 30s, doubling up to 5 minutes. That's the board protecting itself, not a fault — wait it out.
    The PIN cannot be looked up. It's write-only in Settings. If it's lost, someone with laptop access has to set a new one.
  5. Pick the sport.
    This screen opens every time. Tapping the sport that's already current just moves on — nothing restarts. Tapping a different one restarts the board (~8 seconds) and the panel holds the sport name for 3 seconds to confirm.
    ✓ You should see: "Choose the sport", with the current one tagged current.
  6. Pick what to do: Continue game, New game, Show clock, or Delete saved game.
    Continue only appears if the board is holding a match — it survives a power cut or a restart, so if the plug got kicked, this is how you get the score back. Show clock leaves the board on the crest and clock.
    ✓ You should see: the scoreboard — team panels, big + / −, and the tabs Game · Link · History across the top.
  7. Check the wifi symbol at the top-left.
    Green = talking to the board, ready. Red = see Trouble 1.
  8. Type both team names, pick colours. Turn the phone sideways; press the full-screen button.
    Long names shrink to fit automatically — you don't need to abbreviate. If one looks too small, the − / + beside that name raises its ceiling on the panel, immediately.

3 · Running the match — and your live test

Do these in order the first time; it doubles as a full check that everything works.

Do thisOn the board you should see
Tap + on a teamThe point appears and blinks in that team's colour; the serve indicator moves to that side.
Tap − (a correction)The point drops. It does not blink — only real points blink.
Tap a number (score, sets, T/O, sub)A little editor opens — type the exact value, press Set. For fixing a miscount fast. Typed values don't blink.
T/O + for a teamThe panel shows TIME OUT with that team underneath, plus a clock — 30s for indoor volleyball, 60s for beach and basketball. At zero the horn sounds. End it early with ✕ — no horn, deliberately. (Asks you to confirm first.)
T/O + again (to the limit)That counter turns dark red — none left. Indoor gets 2, beach 1, basketball 5.
Sub +The counter blinks; amber one short of the limit, dark red at it. Indoor volleyball only — beach has no subs, basketball shows fouls here instead. (Confirms first.)
Win the setThe winning point blinks, the board holds the final score, and the phone shows a full-screen SET ENDED · Start interval →. There is no cancel — tapping through resets the score, swaps ends, and resets timeouts & subs.
Change of endsThe phone asks "Switch sides?". On yes, the whole panel says COURT SWITCH in gold for ~3s and the ends swap behind it.
Win the matchThe phone offers to end it; the board then shows the result screen — winner in gold, the score, every set. It stays up indefinitely so it outlasts you putting the tablet down. Clear it by starting the next game.
Warm-up / clockFull-screen WARM UP clock (10 min). Horn at zero.
Switch sides (manual)Teams change ends — names, colours, scores all move together.
History tabFinished matches with per-set scores. Export CSV or JSON.
Keep awakeStops the phone sleeping. On the 172.24.1.1 address it needs one tap anywhere on the page to take hold. See the note below.
Settings (gear)Ten sections, each with its own Save button — nothing saves by itself. Includes brightness (0 turns the panel dark while the controller stays up), countdowns, horn, match format, club name, PIN, and links to Diagnostics.

Beach: sets to 21 (15 decider), no substitutions, one timeout each, an amber SWITCH cue on the panel every 7 points (5 in the decider), and an automatic 1-minute technical timeout at 21 total points that swaps ends. Basketball: +1 / +2 / +3 buttons instead of one +, a PERIOD box (Q1–Q4, OT, FINAL) instead of the set line, team fouls (amber at 4, dark red at the 5th = bonus), and the serve arrow becomes possession.

Keep in mind

Turn Keep awake on at the start — but on the 172.24.1.1 address the browser can't hold the screen on by itself, so it uses a silent video that needs one tap on the page to start. If the screen still sleeps, set the tablet's display timeout to Never in Android settings — that always works. Keep the phone on the ledbox_C0270 wifi; wander out of range and the page pauses, walk back and it reconnects. The score is safe: it lives on the board, not the phone, so you can lock the phone, swap phones, or hand over — nothing is lost. More than one phone can be connected; they all show the same score. Keep one person scoring (a second scorer double-counts).

4 · Packing up

  1. Settings → Power → Shut down (confirm).
    Halts the board cleanly — this protects the memory card. Never just pull the plug while it's running.
    ✓ You should see: "Now press the power button to switch it off", and the panel goes dark within a few seconds.
  2. Unplug the board from mains.
    Only after the panel has gone dark. It starts itself next time — nothing to launch by hand.
  3. Put the phone back on its normal wifi / mobile data. Coil the cable.

Just need the board off for a while? Don't shut down — set brightness to 0 in Settings. The panel goes dark and the controller stays up, so you're one tap from being live again.

5 · Troubleshooting

Work down the list. Nine times out of ten it's the first two rows.

ProblemWhat it meansWhat to do
Trouble 1 — "Board not connected"The controller is fine; it can't reach the panel. Your scores are still being recorded. Right after power-on the display takes a few extra seconds — wait ~30s and it clears on its own. Still red after a minute → power-cycle the board (off at the mains, wait 10s, on) and wait ~45s.
Trouble 2 — "…frozen at HH:MM:SS"The opposite problem: your phone has lost the controller. Nothing on screen is live. Don't touch the board — it's probably fine. Check the wifi still says ledbox_C0270 and mobile data is OFF. Walk closer. The page recovers by itself when the wifi comes back.
Trouble 3 — Page won't open at allPhone isn't on the board's wifi, or slipped to mobile data. Check the wifi says ledbox_C0270. Turn mobile data OFF. Reload 172.24.1.1:8890. Wifi not listed at all → the board isn't powered / still booting (wait for the crest).
Trouble 4 — Stuck on the PIN screenWrong PIN, or you've tripped the lockout. "Too many wrong PINs — try again in Ns" is the lockout: 5 tries, then 30s, doubling to 5 minutes. Wait it out. The PIN cannot be recovered from Settings — it's write-only. See Trouble 8.
Trouble 5 — Board stuck on an old scoreThe link dropped and came back. Wait ~20 seconds — the board checks itself and repaints. Don't tap + then −: at set point that + genuinely wins the set and opens a prompt with no cancel.
Trouble 6 — Blank page / missing buttonsStale page in the phone browser. Pull to refresh, or close the tab and scan SCAN 2 again.
Trouble 7 — Nothing worksStart from zero. Off at the mains, count to ten, on again. Wait ~45s for the crest, rejoin the wifi, reopen the page. Your match is not lost — pick Continue game.
Trouble 8 — Dig deeperYou already have the tools, on the phone in your hand. Open the Diagnostics tab (also on the sport picker): temperature, power, disk, memory, network, clock. It carries a live log with search and export — that's what to send. Only then reach for a laptop: systemctl status ledbox-bridge, journalctl -u ledbox-bridge -n 50.

If the board's clock looks wrong, it is. There's no battery clock in it — with no internet it can boot hours or days out, and match history is stamped from it. Diagnostics tells you whether the time is synced.
