What to check

  1. Print page 1 at 100 % / actual size (not “fit to page”). The 50 mm bar must measure 50 mm, the board 90 × 60.
  2. MCP23017, label side up: single row at the top, VCC at the left (checked 13.11). Beep test: one probe on single-row GND, the other on the right end of the inner row, then the outer row. The one that beeps is row A; the drawing says outer.
  3. DS3231, chip side up: the four plain holes at its right end (SCL SDA VCC GND) sit over U2 (checked 13.11: the right-angle header is the other way round and points sideways).
  4. Put the INA226, AOD4184 and button next to the paper where they will sit in the box: are the J-sockets on the right side for their wires?
  5. Tell me what moves; I make the KiCad board from the corrected positions.

Squares = pin 1. Red outline = MCP23017 (plugs in from above). Blue outline = DS3231 (on 4 straight pins, chip side up, cell underneath). Dashed circles = M2.5 holes, keep clear. The INA226 boards, AOD4184, PD board and button are not on the hub; they connect with cables.

Notes

  1. Everything runs at 3.3 V (MCP23017, DS3231, INA226, SHT40). The only 5 V on the hub is the button ring.
  2. I²C pull-ups come from the modules. With all of them on the bus they add up: if the bus is unreliable, cut the I²C pull-up jumper on the STUSB4500 board first.
  3. The I²C ports are JST-XH, not Qwiic: through-hole, easy to solder by hand, and they lock. Wire each pigtail to the module's own pin labels (the INA226 order is VCC SDA SCL ALE GND).
  4. The J1 lead: XH-6 on the hub, six single Dupont housings on the Pi's pins 1, 4, 6, 12, 27, 28 through the tall stacking header.
  5. DS3231: solder 4 straight male pins into its plain SCL SDA VCC GND holes, plastic on the cell side, pins pointing down (with the Pinecil, in the same session as removing the module's charging resistor “201” next to D2). Leave the right-angle header on; put tape over its pins so they touch nothing.
  6. Positions follow ruler photos (±1 mm). Nothing is ordered until the paper check is done.
