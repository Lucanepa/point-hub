Cold start → scoring

  1. Plug the board into mains. Gold POINT HUB splash, then ~45s to the crest with two QR codes.
  2. SCAN 1 → join the wifi. Turn mobile data OFF. The wifi has no internet. That's normal.
  3. SCAN 2 → the controller opens. Bookmark it. Scan it BEFORE you open the page — the QRs vanish from the board once anyone connects.
  4. Enter the PIN if asked. With a PIN set, this covers the whole page. It cannot be looked up.
  5. Choose the sport. Tapping the one already marked current changes nothing. A different one restarts the board — about 8 seconds.
  6. Continue game or New game. "Continue" appears only if the board is holding a match — after a power cut, this gets it back.
  7. Type the team names, pick colours. Phone sideways, then full screen. Long names shrink automatically. No need to abbreviate.
  8. Tap + to score. The point blinks in that team's colour.
Screen keeps sleeping? Tap Keep awake, then tap once anywhere on the page — it needs that tap to take hold. Still sleeping → set the tablet's display timeout to Never in Android settings.

When it goes wrong · work down the list

On screenWhat to do
"Board not connected" The panel is unreachable — your scores are still being saved. Wait 30s. Still there after a minute → power-cycle the board, wait 45s.
"…frozen at HH:MM:SS" Opposite problem: your phone lost the board. Don't touch the board. Check wifi is ledbox_C0270, mobile data OFF, walk closer.
Page won't open Wrong wifi, or it slipped to mobile data. Rejoin ledbox_C0270, data OFF, reload 172.24.1.1:8890.
"Too many wrong PINs" Lockout: 5 tries, then 30s, doubling to 5 min. Wait it out — nothing is broken.
Board stuck on an old score Wait ~20s — it repairs itself. Do not tap + then −: at set point that + really wins the set.
Blank page / no buttons Pull to refresh, or close the tab and scan SCAN 2 again.
Nothing works Mains off, count to ten, on. Wait 45s, rejoin wifi, reopen. Your match is not lost — pick Continue game.
Need to send a report Diagnostics tab (also on the sport picker) → live log → Export.

Packing up

  1. Settings → Power → Shut down, confirm. Never just pull the plug — it protects the memory card.
  2. Wait for the panel to go dark, then unplug. The phone says "Now press the power button to switch it off".
  3. Put your phone back on normal wifi / mobile data.
Only need it off for a while? Don't shut down — Settings → brightness 0. Panel goes dark, controller stays up, one tap to come back.

Good to know

The score is safeIt lives on the board, not your phone. Lock the phone, swap phones, hand over — nothing is lost.
Only one scorerSeveral phones can watch the same match, but two people tapping + double-counts.
Settings don't auto-saveEach section has its own Save button. Leave without pressing it and the change is gone.
The board's clockNo battery clock — with no internet it can be badly wrong. Diagnostics says whether it's synced.
